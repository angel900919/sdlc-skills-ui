# Prompt: Grill Me (interview-to-spec)

_Use before planning a non-trivial feature, to reach shared understanding before any code.
Paste into a fresh session._

> I want to build [brief description]. Interview me in detail, one question at a time, and
> for each question give me your own recommended answer so I can just confirm or correct.
> Ask about technical implementation, UI/UX, edge cases, failure modes, and tradeoffs —
> dig into the hard parts I might not have considered, skip the obvious. Keep going until
> we've covered everything, then write a complete plan to `.ai/plans/NNN-[name].md` using
> the template, with explicit out-of-scope items and vertical-slice tasks.

Then implement in a **fresh session** from the plan.
