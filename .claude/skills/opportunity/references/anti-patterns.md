# Opportunity — anti-patterns (scan the draft before writing)

Each row is a rejection class. If the draft shows the symptom, fix it before writing the artifacts.

| Symptom | Why it fails | Fix |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| "Build feature X" framed as the opportunity | A solution masquerading as an opportunity | Reframe to the need/job it serves; require 2–3 candidate solutions on the tree. |
| Sized top-down only (TAM → a big number) | The #1 sizing red flag; TAM-as-forecast | Size **both** top-down and bottom-up; reconcile the gap; use a defensible SOM with stated assumptions. |
| One precise multi-year ROI figure | False precision on an uncertain bet | Lean case with low/expected/high ranges + sensitivity; revisit as evidence arrives. |
| Only four risks rated | Ethics omitted (the 5th assumption type) | Make ethics a first-class risk with its own assumption and rating. |
| Every risk marked "low", no evidence | Optimism dressed as analysis | Rate importance × evidence honestly; name the riskiest and test it first. |
| No outcome / North Star it serves | Strategy drift — an orphan bet | Anchor to a North Star/OKR from `.ai/strategy`, or record the no-fit as a PIVOT/KILL signal. |
| Invented market size, cost, or quote | Answered an external question from the room | Mark it `TODO:` with an owner, or a flagged assumption; route to `/market-research` or `/research-report`. |
| Gate always says "Persevere" | Rubber-stamping; Pivot/Kill seen as failure | Treat it as a decision — park/kill weak bets cheaply; the kill memo is the most valuable output. |
| AI's sizing/case pasted in as truth | Synthetic confidence, no ownership | The AI drafts and red-teams; the human owns the assumptions, the ratings, and the go/no-go. |
| No "cost of not doing it" | Only the build cost considered | State the downside of inaction; a cheap reversible bet with real upside can clear a lean bar. |
