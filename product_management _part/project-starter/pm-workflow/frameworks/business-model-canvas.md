# Business Model Canvas

A one-page visual template that maps how a business **creates, delivers, and captures value** across **nine building blocks**. Primary source: **Alexander Osterwalder & Yves Pigneur**, *Business Model Generation* (2010 / Strategyzer). Canon: [Conventions §8](../05_Conventions.md). (Ash Maurya's **Lean Canvas** is the startup adaptation.)

## What it's for / when to use
- Sketching and stress-testing the business viability of an opportunity in **P04**, and sanity-checking strategy (**P01**) and GTM (**P11**).
- Getting a whole team to a shared, one-page view of how the business actually works.
- When you need to see the *whole* model at once and spot where the risk/assumptions concentrate.

## The steps (right side = value/desirability, left side = infrastructure/feasibility)
1. **Customer Segments** — who you serve (start here).
2. **Value Propositions** — the job done / pain relieved for each segment.
3. **Channels** — how you reach and deliver to them.
4. **Customer Relationships** — how you get, keep, and grow customers.
5. **Revenue Streams** — how the value is captured (pricing model).
6. **Key Resources** — the assets the model requires.
7. **Key Activities** — what you must do well.
8. **Key Partnerships** — who/what you rely on externally.
9. **Cost Structure** — the major costs; check revenue > cost (viability).
10. **Then test the riskiest assumptions** — it's a living hypothesis canvas, not a finished deliverable.

## Worked micro-example (a niche B2B scheduling tool)
- **Segment:** independent physiotherapy clinics. **Value prop:** fewer no-shows via automated reminders.
- **Channels:** SEO + clinic-software marketplaces. **Relationships:** self-serve + email support.
- **Revenue:** $49/clinic/month. **Key resource:** the reminder/SMS engine. **Key activity:** message deliverability.
- **Partners:** SMS gateway, EHR integrations. **Costs:** SMS, hosting, support → margin positive at ~120 clinics.

## When NOT to use it
- Early-stage startup hunting for problem/solution fit — use the **Lean Canvas** (swaps in Problem, Solution, Key Metrics, Unfair Advantage).
- When you need real market sizing or a financial model — BMC is a sketch ([TAM/SAM/SOM](../03_Frameworks_Map.md) and a business case do that).

## Common mistakes / anti-patterns
- **Static one-time deliverable.** Filling the 9 boxes once and filing it; it should evolve with evidence.
- **Treating it as a forecast** or a finished plan rather than a set of assumptions to test.
- **All boxes, no testing** — completeness without identifying and de-risking the riskiest assumption.
- **Inside-out value props** describing features, not the customer's job/pain.
