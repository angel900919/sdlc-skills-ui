# GTM Plan — Cadence Async Standup (GA)

> Example artifact. Per [`templates/GTM_Plan.md`](../../templates/GTM_Plan.md); skill [`pm-phase-11-launch-gtm`](../../skills/pm-phase-11-launch-gtm/). **Release ≠ launch:** the code shipped behind a flag (staged 5%→100%); this is the GTM moment.

## Launch tier & motion
- **Tier 2** (notable feature, not company-defining) — internal-before-external; PMM-led with a RACI.
- **Motion: hybrid PLG + sales-assist.** Self-serve install via Slack directory; sales-assist triggers when a workspace passes **3 active teams** (a PQL).

## Positioning (Dunford)
- **Competitive alternatives:** the improvised `#standup` Slack thread; an off-hours live standup; heavyweight async tools in a separate app.
- **Unique value:** alignment without a meeting, *inside Slack*, with blockers surfaced for leads.
- **Best-fit segment:** distributed software teams across ≥3 timezones (PER-01/02).
- **Message:** "Standups that respect everyone's timezone — and surface blockers before they cost you a day."

## Pricing & packaging
Free up to 1 team; **Pro** per-active-team/month (usage-aligned, not per-seat — fits the agentic-era shift). Pricing experiment queued post-GA.

## Rollout & readiness
- Staged: 5% → 25% → 100% over 2 weeks, watching MET-01/03/04 + guardrails MET-06/07.
- **Rollback:** feature flag + kill-switch; on-call assigned.
- **Success metrics:** MET-03 activation ≥40% (KR-02) at 30d; **guardrails:** MET-06 opt-out <8%.
- **Enablement:** Sales + Support briefed (RACI); help-center + in-app onboarding live; Legal/privacy sign-off (REQ-SEC-01).

## Gate G9 — Launch Decision (GA)
**Persevere** (2026-05-28). Review at 7/30/90 days; feeds [Growth_Model](../15_Growth/Growth_Model.md) and [Insight_Synthesis](../14_Feedback/Insight_Synthesis.md).
