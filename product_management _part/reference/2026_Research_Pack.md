# 00 — Product Management 2026 Research Pack

A consolidated, citation-rich reference to the durable fundamentals, the genuinely new
2024-2026 shifts, the key frameworks, how AI assists, the leading tools, and the
anti-patterns across the full product lifecycle. Built from 18 source briefs to ground a
world-class PM workflow + skills. Every source URL from the underlying briefs is preserved
per area.

**Cross-cutting meta-shift (true in almost every area):** AI is commoditizing *building*
and the coordination half of the PM job (tickets, grooming, drafting, synthesis). The
scarce resources become **product judgment, strategy, distribution, deep customer
understanding, and taste**. The recurring rule is **"amplify your thinking, don't abdicate
it"** — AI drafts; the human owns the diagnosis, the bet, the trade-offs, the ethics, and
accountability. Outcome-over-output, continuous discovery, and source-grounded evidence are
the connective tissue.

## 1. Strategy & Vision

**Durable fundamentals.** Vision (3-10yr aspirational future) and strategy (the hard
*choices* to get there) are different artifacts; conflating them produces "fluff." Strategy
is choice = focus; deciding what *not* to do is the core act. A real strategy starts with a
**diagnosis** of the single most important obstacle (Rumelt). Strategy must cascade
mission → company strategy → product strategy → roadmap → goals (Product Strategy Stack).
Pick **one North Star** that captures realized customer value (leading indicator of revenue,
in your sphere of influence, with 3-5 input metrics). OKRs *measure* strategy; they are not
the strategy. Give empowered teams *problems + strategic context*, not features. Optimize
outcomes over outputs (escape the build trap). Ground vision in real customer problems
(work backwards).

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** AI absorbs the coordination half of PM, pushing strategy and
product sense to the center. Cagan's "build to learn vs. build to earn" separates
AI-accelerated discovery from delivery. **Distribution is the new bottleneck** as building
gets cheap. The **Product Operating Model** is now the reference standard replacing the
feature factory. Outcome-based Now/Next/Later roadmaps + Opportunity Solution Trees are the
default. Norm for AI strategy work: "amplify your thinking, don't abdicate it."

**Key frameworks.** Product Strategy Stack (Mehta/Reforge); Rumelt's Kernel (Diagnosis →
Guiding Policy → Coherent Action); North Star Framework (Amplitude); Cagan's Empowered
Product Strategy (Focus/Insights/Actions/Management); Working Backwards PR-FAQ (Amazon);
OKRs as the execution/measurement layer; Opportunity Solution Tree (Torres); Business Model
/ Lean Canvas (Osterwalder/Maurya); Escaping the Build Trap / Product Kata (Perri).

**How AI assists.** Drafts narratives/PR-FAQ/OKR trees, synthesizes research and competitive
scans, accelerates "build-to-learn" prototyping, mines feedback into candidate opportunities.
Humans own the diagnosis, the non-goals, taste, customer empathy, ethical trade-offs, and
the bet.

**Leading tools.** Amplitude/Mixpanel/Statsig/Pendo (North Star); Productboard/Aha!/
ProductPlan/Jira Product Discovery/airfocus (strategy & roadmap); Vistaly/Maze/Dovetail
(discovery); Quantive/monday.com/Lattice/WorkBoard (OKRs); Miro/FigJam/Canvanizer (canvases);
ChatGPT/Claude (narrative drafting).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** OKRs *as* strategy; date-locked Gantt feature roadmaps as the
primary plan; vision/values word-soup as "strategy"; output/velocity as success; vanity
North Stars (DAU, registered users, raw revenue); feature lists handed down without context;
BMC as a static one-time deliverable.

## 2. Market & Competitive

**Durable fundamentals.** Define the market from the demand side (people + functional job),
not your product category. Size with **two methods** (top-down for bounds, bottom-up for
credibility) and reconcile within ~15%; bottom-up is the defensible number. Competitive
alternatives start with the **status quo / "do nothing"** (~40% of B2B deals lost to "no
decision"). Positioning is deliberate context (Dunford order: competitive alternatives →
unique attributes → value → best-fit segment → market category), not a tagline. Talk to
buyers — ~85% of CRM loss reasons are wrong; buyer vs. seller explanations align only ~15%.
Separate facts from inference; triangulate sources; tie CI to decisions, not a filed deck.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** GenAI commoditizes scraped public CI → durable advantage shifts to
**proprietary buyer insight** (win/loss, churn interviews) AI can't scrape. **Win/loss is
the backbone of CI** (continuous, CRM-triggered, living battlecards; 97% maintain/grow
investment). Tiered model: live interviews for strategic deals + AI-moderated for the long
tail + surveys for trends; MCP connectors make CI queryable by LLM agents. AI weak-signal
trend detection (~70-85% precision near-term, degrades with horizon). Embedded/in-platform
AI displacing general-purpose AI in research (75%→67%). Category design vs. positioning is a
sequencing decision (positioning first for most $5M-$75M firms).

**Key frameworks.** TAM/SAM/SOM with top-down+bottom-up reconciliation; JTBD market
definition / need-curve sizing (Ulwick); April Dunford positioning (5 components); Win/Loss
4-step (Clozd; interview within ~14 days); Porter's Five Forces (still useful for
structural/capital-intensive industries, update for ecosystems/co-opetition); PESTEL +
AI weak-signal scanning; category/narrative design (reserved for genuine reframers).

**How AI assists.** Synthesizes transcripts/tickets/reviews; AI-moderated win/loss for the
long tail; weak-signal detection; drafts battlecards/competitor summaries; first-pass
TAM/SAM math. Humans own context/brand fit, the market definition and category choice,
validating AI-surfaced trends, strategic interviews, and pressure-testing sizing assumptions.

**Leading tools.** Clozd/Klue/Crayon/Kompyte (win-loss & CI); HG Insights/AlphaSense/
Similarweb/Crunchbase (market intel); Dovetail/Zappi (VoC & trend prediction);
Gartner/Forrester/IDC/Statista + LLMs (sizing); Dunford methodology + Product Marketing
Alliance (positioning).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** "Capture 1% of a $X B TAM"; top-down-only sizing;
product/demographic-defined markets; Porter as the whole toolkit; logging CRM loss reasons
and calling it win/loss; battlecards from competitor websites refreshed quarterly;
positioning that starts from the category you want; "phantom competitors."

**Sources.** https://www.aprildunford.com/post/a-quickstart-guide-to-positioning · https://www.clozd.com/guides/win-loss-analysis · https://www.clozd.com/blog/b2b-competitive-intelligence-strategy-2026 · https://www.thrv.com/blog/how-to-size-a-market · https://jobs-to-be-done.com/the-jtbd-market-definition-canvas-6c60a6f2baf2 · https://www.marketingjournal.org/how-to-define-your-market-using-jobs-to-be-done-anthony-ulwick/ · https://www.icanpitch.com/blog/tam-sam-som-market-sizing-guide · https://hginsights.com/2025/03/07/tam-sam-som-the-complete-guide-to-market-sizing/ · https://www.mtlc.co/revising-porters-five-forces-analysis-in-the-age-of-ai/ · https://timreview.ca/article/1366 · https://www.pitchkitchen.com/blog/category-design-vs-positioning-which-does-your-b2b-company-need · https://www.zappi.io/web/blog/how-ai-predicts-consumer-trends/ · https://www.ideaplan.io/blog/ai-product-management-2026 · https://www.gartner.com/reviews/market/competitive-and-market-intelligence-tools

## 3. Customer Discovery

**Durable fundamentals.** Get out of the building (Blank): a startup searches for a
repeatable, scalable business model (Discovery → Validation → Creation → Company Building).
Continuous discovery is a **cadence, not a project** — Torres's minimum bar is ≥1 customer
interview/week by the team building the product, with discovery and delivery in parallel.
Frequency beats sample size. Interview for **stories, not opinions** ("tell me about the
last time…"). Separate problem space from solution space; don't pitch in a discovery
interview. The **product trio** (PM+design+eng) owns discovery. Synthesize each interview
fresh (interview snapshot). Make recruiting automatic. Tie discovery to an outcome on an OST.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** AI-moderated interviews enable discovery "at scale" (toward ~1 PM
: ~50 conversations/week) — shifting the human role to designing the study and judging
synthesis. Manual transcript coding is effectively dead (AI auto-tagging/summaries are table
stakes). Continuous/always-on cadence beats project-based; the OST is a living weekly layer.
Discovery now routinely includes "what should we do with AI?" (Torres's "Just Now
Possible"). External feedback streams (tickets, reviews, NPS, calls) merge with interviews.
*Continuous Discovery Habits* turns 5 in 2026 — now mainstream canon.

**Key frameworks.** Continuous Discovery Habits (Torres); the Interview Snapshot;
story-based / "tell me about a time" interviewing; Opportunity Solution Tree; Steve Blank
Customer Development; The Mom Test (Fitzpatrick).

**How AI assists.** Moderates/scales routine interviews, transcribes, tags, aggregates
opportunities (claimed time-to-insight cuts; treat as UNVERIFIED). Dovetail-style summaries
compress analysis. Keep an ~80/20 split (AI for routine/high-volume; humans for high-stakes
and foundational ethnography). Humans own bias control, representativeness, rapport, and
challenging convenient conclusions.

**Leading tools.** Perspective AI/BuildBetter (AI-moderated); User Interviews/Respondent/
Sprig (recruiting/intercepts); Dovetail/Maze (synthesis); Enterpret/Anecdote/Productboard
AI (feedback aggregation); FullStory/LogRocket + Amplitude/Mixpanel/Pendo (behavioral);
Miro/Figma/FigJam (OST + snapshot).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Big upfront research then build; counting how many users you
talked to as the metric; direct/speculative questions ("would you use this?"); pitching in
discovery; outsourcing discovery to a separate research team; manual transcript coding as
default; ad-hoc weekly recruiting scrambles.

## 4. User Research & JTBD

**Durable fundamentals.** Research informs decisions, not opinions — tie every study to a
decision it will change. Observe behavior, not just stated preference. Talk to customers
continuously. Frame around the job/outcome, not the solution. Separate problem from solution
space. Triangulate qual (why) + quant (how many). Recruit the right participants and watch
for bias. Small n (~5) is fine for qualitative usability iterations (not quantitative
claims). Make insights findable/reusable (ResearchOps). **Personas and JTBD are
complements**, not rivals.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** AI-assisted synthesis is table stakes but **human review is
non-negotiable** (~97% use AI, 74% say output needs review). Synthetic users are hyped but
distrusted (~8% regular use, ~64% skeptical; OK for screener/concept stimulus, disqualified
for pricing/PMF/demand). Research democratization with governance (split discovery
enablement from strategic research). "Research debt" is the new technical debt. Continuous
discovery + OST mainstream. Thoughtful revival of in-person moderated deep qual. Tool sprawl
(~4+ platforms).

**Two JTBD schools.** **Ulwick/ODI** (jobs-as-activities, quantitative): desired-outcome
statements rated on importance × satisfaction; Opportunity Algorithm `Importance +
max(Importance − Satisfaction, 0)`. **Christensen/Klement** (jobs-as-progress, qualitative):
switch interviews + Forces of Progress (push/pull vs. habit/anxiety). 2026 consensus:
qualitative to *find* the job, ODI to *quantify and prioritize*. **Job stories**: "When
[situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [outcome]" (drops the persona). Personas aren't
dead — demographic-portrait personas are; well-made personas carry goals/motivations.

**Key frameworks.** ODI (Ulwick); Switch interviews + Forces (Christensen/Klement); Job
Stories (Intercom); OST (Torres); continuous interviewing; personas/segmentation (NN/g);
mixed methods / triangulation.

**How AI assists.** Transcription/translation, first-pass coding/clustering, affinity
grouping, survey/guide drafting, descriptive stats, desk research. Humans own thematic
interpretation, hallucination/fact-checking, behavioral observation, sensitive/rapport
interviews, and stakeholder calibration (80% worry stakeholders over-trust AI findings).

**Leading tools.** Dovetail/Notably/Marvin/Condens/Looppanel (synthesis); Perspective AI/
Conveo/Outset/Strella/UserTesting (AI-moderated); User Interviews/Great Question/Respondent
(recruiting); Amplitude/Mixpanel/Pendo/PostHog/Hotjar/FullStory (quant); Statsig/Eppo/
Optimizely (experimentation); Productboard/Maze/Optimal Workshop/Miro (workflow).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** "Personas replace JTBD" / "JTBD killed personas";
demographic-only personas; big-bang phase-gated research; treating qualitative switch
interviews/job stories as quantitatively validated; output/feature-request counting;
trusting AI/synthetic output without review; "5 users is enough" applied to everything.

## 5. Opportunity Assessment

**Durable fundamentals.** Frame the opportunity around a customer problem, not a solution
(if there's only one way to address it, it's a solution masquerading as an opportunity). Tie
every opportunity to one measurable desired outcome. De-risk the **four big risks** before
building: Value, Usability, Feasibility, Business Viability. Make assumptions explicit
(importance × evidence); test the riskiest first. Size with top-down + bottom-up; sizing is
to *prioritize/rank*, not lock a forecast. Prefer a lean, assumption-driven business case.
Outcomes over outputs. Keep evidence fresh and traceable to a customer quote. Recommend,
then decide reversibly (two-way doors).

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** AI-moderated discovery collapses the evidence cycle (~91%; weeks →
days), making the opportunity space a weekly living layer. Research is strategic and
democratized ("essential to all levels" tripled 8%→22%; 80% use AI). "Time-from-signal-to-
shipped" is now a tracked metric. Surveys losing credibility (volume +71% since 2020,
response rates collapsing). **Both-or-nothing market sizing** (top-down-only = #1 red flag).
**Ethics is a first-class assumption** (Torres's 5th type). Lean business cases revisited
per increment.

**Key frameworks.** Cagan/SVPG Opportunity Assessment (10 questions); The Four Big Risks;
Opportunity Solution Tree (Torres); Five Types of Assumptions (Desirability/Viability/
Feasibility/Usability/Ethical); Assumptions Mapping + Test Cards (Strategyzer); TAM/SAM/SOM
(+PAM); RICE / Cost of Delay / Opportunity Scoring; Lean Business Case (SAFe/Strong).

**How AI assists.** Synthesis with citations is the week-one win (every claim links to
source). AI populates the opportunity space; humans choose the target. AI drafts the business
case; humans own assumptions, sensitivity ranges, and the go/no-go. Keep judgment human for
value/viability/ethics/strategy fit; guard against synthetic confidence.

**Leading tools.** Perspective AI/Maze/Dovetail/Kraftful/User Interviews/Great Question
(discovery); Claude/ChatGPT/ChatPRD/Notion AI (synthesis & writing); Productboard/Aha!/
ProductPlan/Miro/Mural/FigJam/Vistaly (mapping); spreadsheet + LLM/TAM-SAM-SOM calculators
(sizing); Strategyzer Test Cards/Maze/Optimizely/Statsig/Eppo (assumption testing).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Heavy single-shot business cases with precise multi-year ROI;
output/feature roadmaps as the plan; treating the OST/assessment as one-time (frozen tree);
opinion-driven/HiPPO prioritization; top-down-only TAM; surveys as primary evidence; only
four risks (ignoring ethics); one solution per opportunity (compare 2-3).

## 6. Roadmap

**Durable fundamentals.** A roadmap is a **strategic communication artifact, not a delivery
contract**. Optimize for outcomes, not output. Certainty decreases with horizon (cone of
uncertainty) — commit features/dates only in "Now." Tie the roadmap to strategy/OKRs;
organize swimlanes by objective/outcome, not team/feature. Prioritize with evidence (RICE/
ICE + discovery), not HiPPO. Make it skimmable (~10s to grasp). Roadmaps are living
documents on a review cadence. Use story mapping to bridge strategy to releasable slices.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** Roadmaps shift from "delivery plan" to **"decision system"** (each
bet names a problem, segment, confidence, assumptions, decision date). AI compresses shipping
so **product judgment is the scarce resource** ("AI can make a team look productive while
building the wrong things"). Dated/timeline roadmaps fail even harder in the AI era. The
two-stream / **agent-as-user** shift (products serve human + agent users; new metrics: task
success rate, human-intervention rate, API reliability). Outcome ownership mainstream but
uneven (54% of PMs still track features/releases). Adaptive multi-cadence planning replaces
annual planning.

**Key frameworks.** Now/Next/Later (Bastow/ProdPad); outcome-based roadmap; theme-based
roadmap; OST extension of Now/Next/Later (Torres); GIST Planning (Gilad: Goals/Ideas/
Step-projects/Tasks); User Story Mapping (Patton); RICE/ICE.

**How AI assists.** Upstream synthesis (summarize tickets, cluster themes, surface
anomalies) — "collate the signals, then make the call yourself." Don't let AI auto-prioritize
by counting requests. AI-drafted artifacts need human editing. Humans own goal-setting,
trade-offs, stakeholder trust, and validating AI confidence scores. Faster shipping raises
the discovery bar.

**Leading tools.** ProdPad/Productboard/Aha!/ProductPlan/Roadmunk/airfocus/Craft.io/
Dragonboat/Jira (roadmapping); BuildBetter.ai/Productboard AI/Figma AI Roadmap Generator
(AI); StoriesOnBoard/Easy Agile/Miro (story mapping); Dovetail/Canny/UserVoice/Hotjar
(feedback); Amplitude/Mixpanel/Statsig/Eppo/Looker/Tableau (analytics).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Timeline/Gantt feature-date roadmaps; roadmap-as-contract /
promising features to sales by date; output as success (velocity/features shipped); big-bang
annual planning locked for a year; HiPPO/vote-count prioritization (incl. naive AI
clustering); over-specifying "Later" like "Now"; one roadmap/one audience; ignoring the
agent/API user class.

## 7. Prioritization

**Durable fundamentals.** Prioritize against a clear outcome/objective, not "good ideas." A
framework **structures the conversation; it does not make the decision** (Intercom: "RICE
scores shouldn't be used as a hard and fast rule"). Garbage in, garbage out — invest in the
estimates, not the formula. Make **Confidence** explicit. **Filter, then rank** (pair a
categorical filter + a scoring model). Match the framework to team maturity and decision
type. Re-prioritize on a cadence with a blank slate (re-run Kano every ~12-18 months).
**Prioritize the opportunity space before solutions.** Keep it transparent and shared.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** **Strategy-first, not framework-first** — the debate is settled and
second-order; prioritize problems/opportunities, not a feature backlog (Cagan). AI-assisted
scoring is mainstream (auto-cluster feedback, link insights, propose scores; PM validates).
Outcome-over-output roadmaps are the default expectation. Kano category drift is tracked
(delighters decay to table-stakes). Growing backlash against SAFe-style WSJF / Cost-of-Delay
(gamed, biased against platform work). People stack-ranking (rank-and-yank) is settled as an
anti-pattern; top-N *priority* stack-ranking is endorsed.

**Key frameworks (cheat sheet).** RICE (reach data + many comparable bets); ICE (early/fast
teams); Value vs Effort 2×2 (quick triage); MoSCoW (fixed-deadline scoping); Kano
(satisfaction strategy); WSJF/Cost of Delay (SAFe/time-critical/platform sequencing);
Opportunity Scoring (Ulwick, under-served needs); Weighted Scoring (many stakeholders,
auditable). Each has documented failure modes (false precision, subjectivity, gaming,
spreadsheet theater).

**How AI assists.** Heavy lifting on inputs (cluster thousands of items, link insights,
draft reach/impact, propose scores; reported ~18h saved per 2-week sprint, ~80% faster
triage). Simulate trade-offs. Humans own the outcome/strategy AI scores against, validating
estimates aren't hallucinated, and strategic/ethical/relationship calls. Anti-pattern:
treating an AI score as objective truth.

**Leading tools.** Productboard/Jira Product Discovery/Aha!/ProductPlan/airfocus/Dragonboat;
Productlift/Featurebase/Canny (feedback+voting); Miro/FigJam (Kano/OST workshops);
Maze/Dovetail (research signals); Sheets (universal RICE/weighted substrate).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Feature-backlog prioritization as the core PM job; treating a
framework score as the decision ("the spreadsheet decided"); HiPPO dressed up with a
framework; forced/stack ranking of people; output/feature-and-date roadmaps; one-and-done
Kano; false-precision scoring; dogmatic WSJF making everything "urgent"; sunk-cost
continuation.

## 8. PRD & Requirements

**Durable fundamentals.** Start with the problem and the outcome, not the solution. A **PRD
documents decisions; it does not make them** (Cagan) — memorialize validated discovery, don't
replace it. Write **testable** requirements (replace "fast/intuitive/secure" with fit
criteria / Given-When-Then). Make scope explicit both ways (in scope AND no-gos). NFRs are
first-class (numeric thresholds). Shorter docs get read; match weight to decision weight. One
living source of truth with changelog/owner. Address the four big risks. Communicate intent,
leave the "how" to the team.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** AI-generated PRD drafting went mainstream (4-8h → <1h editing; PM

## 9. User Stories & Acceptance Criteria

**Durable fundamentals.** A user story is a **placeholder for a conversation, not a spec**
(3 C's: Card/Conversation/Confirmation); the "As a… I want… so that…" template is a starter,
not the deliverable. **INVEST** is the quality bar (refinement checklist, not a gate). Slice
**vertically** (end-to-end value), never horizontally (no frontend/backend stories). Write
AC collaboratively (Three Amigos) before dev. Use **Given/When/Then** declaratively (what,
not how). Split with proven patterns. Keep the whole in view with story mapping (Patton).
DoD = shared quality bar for the Increment; AC = per-story behavior — they differ. Maintain
Epic → Feature → Story → Task.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** AI drafts the backlog; humans curate (Cohn's Jan 2026 prompt pack
endorses AI for stories/AC/Gherkin and INVEST evaluation — but "won't replace talking to
users or product sense"). AC becoming **executable test scaffolding** (AI QA agents tag
Gherkin, surface missing negative/boundary/OWASP/WCAG cases). **Probabilistic forecasting
(Monte Carlo over throughput) displacing story-point velocity**; flow metrics + Little's Law.
**#NoEstimates** matured into "right-sizing + flow." Outcome-over-output reframes stories.
**Definition of Ready increasingly called an anti-pattern** (a rigid gate; a light readiness
conversation is fine).

**Key frameworks.** INVEST (Wake); Gherkin/Given-When-Then (BDD); Story Mapping (Patton);
SPIDR / Humanizing Work splitting patterns; Three C's + Three Amigos; DoD/(optional) DoR;
Epic→Feature→Story→Task; Monte Carlo / flow-based forecasting.

**How AI assists.** Drafts stories at scale, generates AC (list/Gherkin), surfaces edge/
negative/boundary cases, evaluates against INVEST, reformats to GWT, generates automated
tests. Humans own user interviews, prioritization/trade-offs, product judgment, org context,
and validating AI edge cases/value claims. Watch-outs: generic stories, hallucinated AC,
false confidence, over-trusting AI test coverage.

**Leading tools.** Jira/Linear/Azure DevOps/Shortcut (backlog); Productboard/Aha!/Jira
Product Discovery (discovery); StoriesOnBoard/Easy Agile/Avion/Miro (story mapping);
ChatGPT/Claude/Notion AI/Copilot4DevOps/TestStory.ai (AI drafting/test gen); Cucumber/
SpecFlow/Behave (BDD); ActionableAgile/Nave/55degrees (flow metrics & Monte Carlo).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Treating "As a… I want…" as a mandatory rigid spec; exhaustive
upfront AC by a BA/PO in isolation; horizontal/technical splitting; a strict gated DoR;
comparing/normalizing story points/velocity across teams or as a KPI; equating points with
hours / estimation theater; output-based feature-factory roadmaps; assuming every item must
be estimated.

## 10. Agile Delivery & Backlog

**Durable fundamentals.** Empirical process control (transparency/inspection/adaptation on a
short cadence). **Optimize flow, not utilization** (Little's Law: cycle time = WIP /
throughput → start less, finish more). Cross-functional teams own a problem end-to-end. Small
batches + short feedback loops reduce risk. The backlog is living; refine just-in-time (top
~10-20 items). Estimation is for shared understanding/forecasting, not contracts. DoR/DoD set
the quality bar. **Measure to learn, never to judge** (no ranking individuals/teams by
velocity). Fit method to work (Scrum/Kanban/Scrumban). Sustainable pace is a leading
indicator of delivery health.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** Outcome-over-output is the default operating model (Cagan;
feature factory = anti-pattern). Continuous discovery + dual-track with a **product trio** is
the assumed baseline. **Flow metrics displacing velocity**; DORA re-architected (2024 added
Rework Rate; MTTR replaced by Failed Deployment Recovery Time; 2025 = six dimensions). AI is
"the great amplifier" (~90% of devs use AI; throughput up, but AI *negatively* correlates
with stability without safety nets → DORA AI Capabilities Model). **SAFe criticism is
mainstream**, with a clear shift toward hybrid/homegrown scaling (~74%); raw-adoption survey
data is mixed. AI-assisted backlog/sprint work is operational. Probabilistic
forecasting rising. Empowerment is structural — most teams still aren't truly empowered.

**Key frameworks.** Scrum; Kanban + flow metrics (WIP/Cycle Time/Work Item Age/Throughput);
Scrumban; dual-track agile; Continuous Discovery Habits/OST (Torres); empowered teams /
Product Operating Model (Cagan); DORA metrics; Flow Framework (Kersten); WIP limits +
Little's Law; SAFe (with caveats); Monte Carlo forecasting.

**How AI assists.** Backlog & story authoring; discovery synthesis; backlog hygiene &
dependency/bottleneck prediction; forecasting; ~90% dev coding-tool adoption. Humans own
prioritization/trade-offs/outcome definition, guarding delivery stability (AI amplifies what
a team already is), validating AI output (~30% distrust AI code), and avoiding the AI feature
factory.

**Leading tools.** Jira/Linear/Azure DevOps/Shortcut/ClickUp/monday.com (boards); Jira
Product Discovery/Productboard/Aha!/Miro/StoriesOnBoard (discovery/roadmap); Dovetail/Maze/

(flow/DORA); Optimizely/Statsig/Eppo/Amplitude (experimentation); GitHub Copilot/Atlassian

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Velocity as a productivity/comparison metric or target; story
points as a commitment; feature factory / output roadmaps; agile theater / zombie scrum /

## 11. Launch & GTM

**Durable fundamentals.** **Separate "release" (engineering, staged, reversible) from
"launch" (marketing/GTM moment)** — conflating them is the root of most launch risk. Tier
launches by impact (1/2/3); cap Tier 1 at ~2-3/quarter. Positioning is the foundation
(Dunford's 5 components) and comes before messaging/campaigns. Lead with "why now" + customer
value before "what's new." Launch is cross-functional and PMM-led (RACI; internal before
external). Run a go/no-go + pre-mortem. Define 2-3 success metrics; review at 7/30/90 days.
Validate messaging with real customers. De-risk releases technically (flags, % rollout,
canary, kill switch). Match GTM motion to complexity/ACV.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** **The big-bang launch is dying** (continuous incremental rollout
behind flags is the default). **Hybrid PLG+SLG dominates** (67% of hybrids hit NRR vs 58%
pure-PLG). **Agentic PLG** (activation = "first successful output"; AI agents are a fast-rising
share of dev-tool signups — Netlify reported ~13× growth in agent-driven signups, though exact
proportions are contested). Pricing shifting per-seat → usage → outcome/credit (Intercom Fin = $0.99 per
*successful* resolution). **Machine-readable GTM** (buyer-side AI agents screen pricing;
expose MCP/agent-readable APIs). Design partners as a distinct pre-launch motion. Guarded
releases with automated progressive rollout (LaunchDarkly GA 2025). AI-assisted launch ops.

**Key frameworks.** Launch Tier (1/2/3); April Dunford Positioning (5 components + 5 steps) &
Sales Pitch; GTM Motion Decision (PLG/SLG/Hybrid/Community); Progressive Delivery / release
rings (internal→canary→beta→GA); RACI; pre-mortem + go/no-go; Design Partner Program (vs
beta); Pricing models (per-seat/usage/outcome/hybrid/credit).

**How AI assists.** Drafts positioning/messaging/copy/battlecards (humans own the category &
best-fit bets); synthesizes beta/ticket/call feedback; AI-native activation; monitors rollout
metrics + auto-rollback (humans set thresholds & own go/no-go); buyer-side agents evaluate
pricing (humans own pricing strategy); generates checklists/RACI/timelines (humans own
alignment & internal-before-external).

**Leading tools.** Productboard/Aha!/ProductPlan/LaunchNotes/PMA templates (launch ops);

## 12. Analytics & KPIs

**Durable fundamentals.** Measure value delivered, not activity. Pair **one outcome metric
with 3-5 input metrics you can move** (breadth/depth/frequency/efficiency). Distinguish
leading (act early) from lagging (confirm) indicators and instrument both. **Every metric
must change behavior** (Cutler's vanity test). Always show context (comparison/ratio/caveat);
prefer rates over cumulative totals. Be explicit about the proxy. Protect with **guardrail
metrics** (2-3). Instrument deliberately from a **tracking plan**. Enforce event taxonomy &
governance. Match the framework to the question (AARRR/HEART/North Star are complementary).
Triangulate quant + qual.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** **Metric trees** moved from whiteboard to native feature (Mixpanel
launched in-platform Metric Trees, Aug 2025). **Conversational, agentic analytics** replaces
dashboard hunting (NL querying via MCP into Claude/ChatGPT/Cursor; always-on agents surface
anomalies — "autonomous analytics"). The **all-in-one stack** converged (PostHog bundles
analytics+replay+flags+experiments+surveys; Amplitude/Mixpanel each added the other's
features). Tiered **success/guardrail/diagnostic** metric design is standard.
**"Data-informed, not data-driven"** is mainstream. HEART honesty patch for mandatory
software. Warehouse-native/composable analytics is a real option.

**Key frameworks.** North Star Framework (1 output + 3-5 inputs); metric tree (component vs
influence links); AARRR (McClure); HEART (Google) via Goals-Signals-Metrics; leading vs
lagging; guardrail metrics; success/guardrail/diagnostic tiering; Cutler's vanity-metric test;
tracking plan + taxonomy + governance.

**How AI assists.** NL querying (removes SQL/funnel barrier); proactive/autonomous insight
surfacing; AI-built metric trees & summaries; instrumentation hygiene (tracking-plan audit,
taxonomy-drift flags). Humans own defining "value"/the North Star, causal interpretation
(LLMs conflate correlation/causation), guardrails/ethics/rollback thresholds, and qualitative
grounding.

**Leading tools.** Amplitude/Mixpanel/PostHog/Heap/Pendo/Kubit/June (analytics); Statsig/
Eppo/Optimizely/GrowthBook/Spotify Confidence (experimentation/flags); Segment/RudderStack/

decisions; output/feature-count roadmap metrics; optimizing one metric without guardrails;

KPI without behavioral grounding; retention reported without cohorts.

## 13. Experimentation

**Durable fundamentals.** Write a **falsifiable hypothesis** first ("We believe [change]
causes [effect] for [segment], measured by [metric], because [insight]"). Pre-register the
design (primary metric, MDE, alpha, power, sample size) before launch. Do a **power analysis**
up front. One primary decision metric (Kohavi's OEC) + 2-3 guardrails + diagnostics. Run for
full business cycles. Verify trustworthiness (A/A, SRM check). **Don't peek naively** in
fixed-horizon tests. Correct for multiple comparisons. Decision rule = primary wins AND no
guardrail breached. Expect most experiments to lose or be flat.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** **Platform consolidation** (Datadog acquired Eppo (terms undisclosed; ~$220M per press reports) → Datadog
Experiments; OpenAI acquired Statsig ~$1.1B then Amplitude took over the brand; VWO+AB Tasty
merged) → weigh roadmap/ownership risk. **Sequential testing / always-valid p-values are the
mainstream default** (mSPRT/group-sequential; Booking.com runs 1,000+ simultaneously, ~30%
shorter). AI-assisted experimentation in everyday workflow (drafts hypotheses, ranks backlog,
generates variants, writes readouts; AI "digital-twin" predictions as variance-reduction
covariates). **Measuring AI features breaks classic A/B** (dynamic variants, self-
contaminating loops, proxy-metric blindness, novelty inflation → permanent holdouts,
interleaving, response-quality metrics). **CUPED variance reduction is standard** (~30-40%
fewer users). Warehouse-native experimentation matured.

**Key frameworks.** Hypothesis template; OEC (Kohavi); primary/guardrail/diagnostic taxonomy;
power analysis; frequentist fixed-horizon; sequential (mSPRT/group-sequential); Bayesian;
CUPED; multi-armed bandits/Thompson sampling; interleaving; holdouts; one-way vs two-way
door; SRM/A/A checks; ICE/PIE for the backlog. **When NOT to A/B test:** low traffic,
irreversible/strategic decisions, obvious/compliance changes, no clean metric or power.

**How AI assists.** Drafts/prioritizes hypotheses, generates variants, summarizes results,
flags anomalies/SRM/guardrail breaches, powers variance reduction, early agent-driven
experimentation. Humans own which experiments matter (the real bottleneck), the OEC and
trade-offs, guardrails/ethics/ship calls, and interpreting borderline/AI-feature results.

**Leading tools.** Statsig, Datadog Experiments (ex-Eppo), Optimizely, GrowthBook, PostHog,
VWO/AB Tasty, LaunchDarkly, Split (Harness), Amplitude Experiment, Mixpanel, Kameleoon +
power/sample-size calculators.

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Peeking (stopping at first p<0.05) without sequential
correction; significance on tiny samples with no power analysis; conflating statistical with
business significance; arbitrary/sub-weekly durations; "Bayesian = free license to peek";
A/B testing everything (incl. one-way-door/compliance); uncorrected multiple comparisons /
cherry-picking; dropping guardrails for velocity, or blind data-worship ignoring qual.

## 14. Feedback Management

**Durable fundamentals.** Collecting feedback is worthless unless you **close the loop**
(~95% collect, ~10% act, ~5% tell customers — figures UNVERIFIED). Run **two loops**: inner
(resolve the individual fast and tell them) and outer (aggregate to root-cause themes that
change the product). Speed of follow-up (24-48h) drives retention. No single metric — **CSAT
(touchpoint), CES (effort), NPS (relationship)** triangulated, each paired with open-text
"why." Capture feedback in context (in-product microsurveys). Centralize in one searchable
repository linked to customer/ARR/segment. A consistent, governed **tagging taxonomy** makes
feedback analyzable. Feedback is **raw material for discovery, not a backlog of orders.** Most
signal is unsolicited/unstructured (~80%). Show customers what changed.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** AI feedback synthesis at scale is the default (Enterpret/Cycle
auto-cluster all channels, answer NL questions with sourced verbatims in <1 min).
**Adaptive/auto-learned taxonomies** replace hand-maintained tag trees. NPS criticism
mainstream — but academia "qualifies rather than discredits" it (correlates ~0.97 with
satisfaction; comparable to top-box CSAT; the "one number you need to grow" claim is not
supported). "**Feedback ops**" emerging inside Product Ops. Support-ticket mining moved from
sampling to full-corpus AI analysis. Feedback-to-roadmap binding becoming closed-loop and
automated. Continuous discovery reframed feedback as inspiration mapped onto an OST. AI
hallucination risk makes human-in-the-loop structural.

**Key frameworks.** Inner Loop / Outer Loop; NPS / CSAT / CES; feedback taxonomy (type × area
× source × segment); Opportunity Solution Tree; JTBD lens; continuous discovery; support-
ticket/call mining; closed-loop SLA (24-48h).

**How AI assists.** Synthesis at scale; adaptive taxonomy & auto-tagging; NL querying with
sourced answers; loop-closing assistance (drafts personalized replies, routes themes). Humans
own prioritization/trade-offs, hallucination/over-trust checks (source-grounding), context
(frequency ≠ importance; one enterprise renewal can outweigh hundreds of free-tier asks), and
the conversation itself.

**Leading tools.** Enterpret/Cycle (customer intelligence AI); Productboard/Cycle/Aha!/Canny
(feedback-to-roadmap); Sprig/Pendo/Survicate/Refiner (in-product microsurveys); Qualtrics/
Medallia/Thematic/Zonka/Hotjar (VoC analytics); Dovetail/Maze (research repos); Zendesk/
Intercom/Freshdesk/Front/Salesforce + Gong/Chorus/Modjo (mined sources).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** NPS as "the one number you need to grow"; a satisfaction score
with no open-text/follow-up; building whatever is requested most (feature factory by request);
feedback scattered across inboxes/Slack/spreadsheets; manually reading a sample of tickets;
collecting feedback but never telling customers what changed; annual relationship surveys as
the primary listening; over-tagging / ungoverned taxonomy sprawl; blindly trusting AI themes.

## 15. Growth / PLG

**Durable fundamentals.** **Retention is the core of the growth model** (a flattening
retention curve = real PMF). **Activation (the aha moment)** is the highest-leverage step;
distinguish the felt aha moment from the measurable activation event. In PLG the product is
the primary engine (minimize time-to-value). Growth is a system: `(Acquisition + Retention +
Monetization) × Defensibility`. Instrument everything (event-based analytics). Validate with
causal experiments, not correlation. Onboarding should be product-led, friction-light (one
action toward the aha moment, not a product tour). Match monetization to traffic/TTV/pricing
architecture. A single North Star aligns the org around value.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** **Growth loops replaced the funnel** as the default model
(compounding vs leaking; funnels silo channels/product/monetization). **Retention/NRR has
overtaken acquisition** (top performers NRR 120%+ → ~2.5x faster growth). PLG matured into
**hybrid "full-stack" GTM** (~67% above $10M ARR; PQLs convert ~25-30% vs ~5-10% MQLs). The
**measurement gap** is the defining problem (58% use PLG, only 34% track activation).
**AI-native PLG ("PLG 2.0"): distribution by output-sharing** (Cursor ~$2B ARR in ~3yr;
Lovable ~$200M in ~12mo). AI changes the *bottlenecks* of loops, not the fundamentals
(Balfour). AI personalizes onboarding in real time. **Reverse trials** emerge.
"PLG 3.0"/agentic adoption is the forward bet ("agents don't read tooltips").

**Key frameworks.** Growth Loops (Reforge); Growth Equation; Engagement→Retention→
Monetization loop; Activation event + Aha moment + TTV; Setup→Aha→Habit (Balfour); North
Star (acquisition/retention/monetization types); AARRR (as vocabulary, paired with loops);
Hook model (apply ethically); Freemium / Free Trial / Reverse Trial / Usage-based; PQL /
Product-Led Sales. **2026 benchmarks (directional):** activation ~25-40% in 7-14 days
(<20% = problem); opt-out trials convert ~48.8% vs ~18.2% opt-in; trials ≤7d ~40.4% vs >61d
~30.6%; free-trial ~2-3x freemium conversion; NRR 120%+ ≈ 2.5x faster growth.

**How AI assists.** Personalizes onboarding/activation in real time; removes loop bottlenecks
(content/copy/personalization/eng); drafts and runs more experiments. Humans own the
activation event/value hypothesis, strategy/brand/channel-fit/defensibility, statistical
rigor/guardrails, building genuine habit (AI products churn on novelty), and avoiding
manipulative dark patterns.

**Leading tools.** Mixpanel/Amplitude/Heap/PostHog (analytics); Statsig/Eppo/Optimizely/
LaunchDarkly (experimentation); Pendo/Userpilot/Appcues/Chameleon/WalkMe (onboarding);
Pocus/Endgame/Correlated/HubSpot (PQL); Stripe/Metronome/Orb (usage billing); Segment/
RudderStack (CDP); ChartMogul/Maxio (NRR); Reforge/Growth Unhinged (education).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Funnel-only thinking; acquisition-first / "build it and they
will come"; vanity metrics (signups/pageviews/downloads); product/channel/monetization silos;
generic one-size onboarding tours; defaulting to freemium with no value model; "pure PLG, no
sales ever" dogma; growth "hacking" as a bag of tricks; manipulative engagement / dark
patterns as "retention."

## 16. Product Ops

**Durable fundamentals.** Product ops is an **enabling function, not a decision-maker** —
it surrounds PMs with data, insights, process, and tooling so they spend more time on the
product. It helps the product *function* scale; PMs still own decisions. Single source of
truth for data beats scattered dashboards. Democratize customer insight (shared searchable
repository). Standardize the "how," not the "what." A small connected tool stack (3-5) beats
sprawl. Right-size to org maturity (~past 150 people / multiple teams). Measure product ops by
the outcomes it enables. It's the connective tissue across functions.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** Product ops moved from "emerging" to **"embedded"** (96% have some
form; ~50% run dedicated teams). Mandate shifting from process orchestration to **strategic
enablement** (42% expect strategic-partner status within a year). Codified as a discipline
(Perri & Tilles, 2023; anchors Cagan's product operating model). AI reframing the role around
the **data/feedback loop** and "adaptive roadmapping" + AI-infrastructure governance (~50%
cite AI as key). But automation maturity is low (only 7% high automation; 47% treat data
reporting as core). **Role clarity is the #1 unsolved problem.** Cross-functional integration
still shallow (67% prioritize PM collaboration; only 15% fully integrated with Sales/CS).

**Key frameworks.** The three pillars (Perri & Tilles: business data & insights · customer &
market insights · shared processes/practices); practitioner restatement (data/insights ·
process/practices · tools/systems); four-pillar variants (Mind the Product / Reforge);
rituals & cadences (weekly retros → product reviews → monthly ops reviews → quarterly
planning/QBRs); product operating model as destination; ProductOps vs RevOps (build vs
sell/monetize); when-to-invest signals.

**How AI assists.** Feedback/research synthesis at scale; automated trustworthy metrics
reporting; adaptive roadmapping from live telemetry; AI-infrastructure governance; tooling
consolidating into AI-native command centers. Humans own metric definitions and "good,"
interpreting the "why," prioritization, validating AI reports, and the ethics/quality bar.

**Leading tools.** Productboard/Aha!/Productlift/Featurebase (feedback→roadmap); Amplitude/
Mixpanel/Pendo/PostHog (analytics); Jira/Linear/Atlassian (delivery); Dovetail (research);
Notion/Confluence/Slack (docs); ChatPRD/Aha!/Stackby AI + LLMs (AI-assisted PM).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Product ops as a glorified PMO / status-reporting function;
process for process's sake (process theater); letting product ops make product decisions;
"hire product ops to fix product" (standing it up too early); conflating Product Ops with
RevOps/Sales Ops; no measurement of product ops' own impact (21% have none); tool sprawl
(8 loosely related tools vs 3-5 integrated).

## 17. Portfolio & Sunset

**Durable fundamentals.** Portfolio management is **continuous resource allocation**, not a
once-a-year review — decide across all products what to start/scale/sustain/fix/sunset under
constraints, inheriting company strategy. Allocate by strategic value and capacity, not who
lobbies hardest. Segment and rationalize the portfolio. Manage every product against an
explicit lifecycle stage (Development→Introduction→Growth→Maturity→Decline). **Sunsetting is
a deliberate multi-month process** (distinguish sunset runway from deprecation/EOL; estimate
the window, then double it). **Communication is the single biggest determinant** of a clean
sunset (5-7 touchpoints, multi-channel; transparent why/timeline/migration path). Give a
viable destination, not just an exit. Maintain quality/support during runway. Data has its own
lifecycle (GDPR retention/erasure). Run a post-sunset retrospective.

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** Portfolio decisioning becoming continuous and AI-augmented (AI
scores initiatives for value/risk/capacity → start/pause/stop). Enterprise SPM platforms ship
**predictive/prescriptive/agentic** AI with explainability. **"Strategy first, AI second"**
guardrail (anchor to business outcomes, not AI-adoption rates). Outcome-over-output reframes
the portfolio roadmap. Classic BCG matrix is now a screening tool augmented by GE-McKinsey
9-box / ROIC / scenario planning. **Regulators expect automated deletion + data
classification at EOL and are auditing it** (EDPB 2025 erasure framework: 764 controllers
audited; biggest gaps = no systematic classification, no automated deletion; test/dev/backup
in scope). Usage analytics make sunset candidates objective (but investigate context first).

**Key frameworks.** Product Life Cycle; BCG Growth-Share Matrix (screen only); GE-McKinsey
9-box; outcome-over-output prioritization; **5D Sunset Strategy** (Diagnose→Design→Declare→
Deliver→Debrief); ProductPlan 10-step EOL checklist; phased feature-sunset sequence; set exit
criteria / pre-mortem to beat sunk cost (escalation of commitment, Staw 1976).

**How AI assists.** Merges fragmented data (ERP/CRM/analytics/support) into unified portfolio
views; detects overlap/cannibalization/hidden costs; runs NL "what-if" scenarios + constraint
optimization; agentic AI recommends start/pause/stop continuously; drafts sunset comms/
migration guides; triages high-touch accounts. Humans own strategy/weighting/ethics and the
final start/scale/sunset call, stress-test AI against qualitative insight, demand
explainability, and own kill decisions, customer trust, and data-deletion accountability.

**Leading tools.** Planisware/ServiceNow SPM/Planview/Epicflow/Celoxis/monday.com/Businessmap
(SPM); Productboard/Aha!/airfocus/ProductPlan/Jira Product Discovery (PM); Pendo/Amplitude/
Mixpanel/AbTasty (usage analytics + comms); ChatGPT/Claude/Gemini + Neo4j/TigerGraph
(interdependency) + optimization solvers; Power BI/Tableau/ThoughtSpot (BI); Segment/
Snowflake/BigQuery/CDPs (data); standardized `Sunset`/`Deprecation` HTTP headers (APIs).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** BCG matrix as a complete strategy (reflexively dumping "Dogs");
annual/quarterly-only reviews via static spreadsheets; output/feature-count roadmaps; short
abrupt deprecation notices (30-day kill, esp. developer-facing); cutting support/quality the
moment a sunset is announced; sunk-cost / escalation-of-commitment; treating shutdown as the
finish line and ignoring data (manual-only deletion is an audited gap); killing on a single
low-usage number without diagnosing discoverability/usability.

## 18. Stakeholder Management

**Durable fundamentals.** A PM **leads through influence, not authority** — credibility comes
from expertise, reliable delivery, and trusted relationships. Map stakeholders before
managing them (Mendelow Power/Interest grid). Make decision rights explicit (RACI: exactly
one Accountable). Tailor the message (executives want decision + trade-off + ask up front =
BLUF; engineers want context/constraints). Manage up with a cadence, not ad hoc. Document the
why (decision logs, risk registers — increasingly a regulatory expectation).

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** "Stakeholder management" reframed as **collaboration, not control**
— treating colleagues as stakeholders to be "managed" is itself a feature-factory symptom;
shift to shared problems/context/co-ownership (SVPG). **Influence is now an explicitly taught
AI-era skill** (Lenny's 2026 "AI-Native PM" pillars: AI workflows, becoming technical,
product sense & influence). **Prototyping has overtaken spec-writing as the cheapest way to
align stakeholders** (Claude Code/Cursor/Replit/Lovable/Figma Make → "prototype it" beats
"write a spec" for exec buy-in).

**Key frameworks.** RACI; Power/Interest (Mendelow) grid; stakeholder salience / laddering
(power/legitimacy/urgency); BLUF executive comms; influence loop / managing-up cadence.

**How AI assists.** Drafts tailored updates per audience, summarizes long threads, preps
meeting briefs, builds prototypes for buy-in. Humans own stakeholder trust/relationships
(inherently human) and the strategic narrative.

**Leading tools.** Productboard/Aha!/Notion AI/Confluence+Atlassian Rovo/Coda/Miro/ChatPRD
(stakeholder/comms/docs); Cursor/Replit/Lovable/Bolt/v0/Figma Make (prototyping for buy-in).

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** "Stakeholder management" as managing up via persuasion theater
(symptom of a feature factory); RACI with multiple "A"s; output-only exec updates ("we
shipped X") — lead with outcome/trade-off/ask (BLUF).

## 19. AI-Assisted PM

**Durable fundamentals.** AI is a tool that *amplifies* the PM, not a replacement for
judgment. Output is a **draft to be checked**, not a source of truth; the PM remains
accountable for accuracy. AI changes the *inputs* to prioritization, not the prioritization
*logic*. Keep a human accountable for consequential decisions (human-in-the-loop, audit
trails, override/rollback).

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** **AI is now operational across the PM lifecycle, not experimental**
— synthesize research, draft PRDs/user stories, score and de-dupe backlogs, draft roadmaps,
query analytics in natural language. **"AI PM" is a distinct, in-demand role** (AI-fluency
job demand grew ~7x in two years; 40,000+ "AI Product Manager" roles; US comp ~$150k-$230k —
treat exact figures as approximate/UNVERIFIED). Prototyping overtakes spec-writing. The PM's
edge becomes strategy, taste, distribution, and customer understanding — the parts AI assists,
not replaces.

**Key frameworks.** AI-in-the-loop workflow design (map where AI can draft/summarize/route,
then restructure so humans review rather than start from zero); the recurring "amplify your
thinking, don't abdicate it" rule; the human-in-the-loop split applied per lifecycle area.

**How AI assists (lifecycle map).** Discovery & research synthesis; documentation (PRDs,
stories, OKRs, release notes, decks); prioritization (ingests CRM/support/usage/NPS →
evidence-based scores); stakeholder comms; prototyping for buy-in; analytics (NL query +
anomaly detection). **Humans own:** strategy/vision/the bet; prioritization trade-offs and
ethics; stakeholder trust; responsible-AI accountability; verification.

**Leading tools.** ChatGPT/Claude/Claude Code/Microsoft Copilot/Gemini (assistants); ChatPRD
(PRDs); Cursor/Replit/Lovable/Bolt/v0/Figma Make (prototyping); Dovetail/Sprig/LogRocket/Maze
(research synthesis); Mixpanel/Amplitude/Pendo (NL analytics); Atlassian Intelligence/Rovo,
Notion AI (embedded). MCP increasingly connects analytics/CI/feedback to LLM agents.

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Pasting AI output into a PRD/decision without verification;
treating an AI-generated score/theme as objective truth; using AI to auto-prioritize by
counting requests; over-reliance that crowds out customer conversations and product sense;
treating "use AI" as a strategy rather than a capability.

## 20. Responsible Product

**Durable fundamentals.** **Privacy and accessibility are requirements, not afterthoughts** —
"data protection by design and by default" is a legal obligation (GDPR Art. 25), and WCAG
must be designed in. Build for the full range of users (WCAG POUR: Perceivable, Operable,
Understandable, Robust). Keep a human accountable for consequential automated decisions
(oversight, audit trails, override/rollback). **Trust is a feature** (fairness, reliability,
safety, transparency compound; one breach/biased output/inaccessible flow erodes trust faster
than features build it). Document the why (decision logs, risk registers — a regulatory
expectation).

**New 2024-2026 shifts.** **The EU AI Act is live and being re-sequenced** — prohibited-
practice and GPAI rules already apply (Feb/Aug 2025); the "Digital Omnibus" (proposed 19 Nov
2025, agreed 7 May 2026) postponed most high-risk obligations (Annex III standalone → 2 Dec
2027; Annex I product-embedded → 2 Aug 2028) **but kept Article 50 transparency duties
(disclose AI interaction / label AI-generated content) from 2 Aug 2026**. **Accessibility
became hard law in the EU** — the European Accessibility Act has been enforceable since 28
June 2025 (firms 10+ employees, >€2M turnover); EN 301 549 currently maps to WCAG 2.1 AA, with
an update to WCAG 2.2 in progress. **Responsible AI moved from one-time checks to continuous
monitoring** (bias, model drift, performance anomalies across the lifecycle).

**Key frameworks.** Privacy by design (GDPR Art. 25); WCAG 2.2 POUR + EN 301 549; NIST AI Risk
Management Framework (Govern/Map/Measure/Manage); EU AI Act risk tiers (prohibited/high-risk/
limited-risk transparency/minimal); trust & safety harms taxonomy; ISO/IEC 42001.

**How AI assists.** Drafts DPIAs, accessibility checklists, risk classifications, and policy
docs; continuous bias/drift monitoring tooling. **Humans own** bias review, the privacy/DPIA
decision, the accessibility sign-off, the EU AI Act risk classification and human-oversight
design — AI can assist drafting but cannot be the accountable party.

**Leading tools.** axe DevTools (Deque)/WAVE/Lighthouse/Level Access (accessibility);
OneTrust/TrustCloud/Credo AI (privacy/AI governance); frameworks NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001;
Microsoft Responsible AI guidance.

**Outdated / anti-patterns.** Treating accessibility as an optional QA checklist or post-
launch fix (it's enforceable EU law); treating privacy as a legal-team problem handled at the
end; assuming the EU AI Act is "future, not now" (Article 50 bites Aug 2026); one-time
pre-launch fairness/bias review (expect continuous monitoring); pasting AI output into a
decision without verification or human accountability.
