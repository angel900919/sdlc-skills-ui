## 2. Participant & screening
- **Target participant:** TODO: <segment + screening criteria — see Discovery_Plan.md §2>

## 3. Consent & recording script (read verbatim — Responsible-product floor)
> "Thanks for your time. With your permission I'd like to **record** this so I don't miss anything — the recording is used only for our research and stored securely; you can stop at any time. Is that okay?"
<!-- Do not proceed without explicit consent. GDPR Art. 25 — privacy by design on participant data. -->

## 4. Warm-up (build rapport, set the frame)
- "Tell me a bit about your role and a typical <day/week> when <context>."
<!-- Goal: relax the participant and locate them in the real context — not to qualify your idea. -->

## 5. Core story prompts (past behaviour — "tell me about the last time…")
<!-- Anchor on a real, recent, specific episode. Past behaviour predicts; hypotheticals lie. -->
- "Tell me about the **last time** you <faced the situation / had to get the job done>."
- "Walk me through what happened, step by step — what did you do first?"
- "What were you using / who did you turn to?"
- "When did you first realise you needed to deal with this?"  <!-- surfaces the trigger / struggling moment -->
- "What had you been doing **before** that stopped working?"  <!-- Switch: push of the current situation -->

## 6. Probes (go deeper — job, struggle, workaround, "better")
<!-- Use silence. Ask "why" and "tell me more" rather than introducing new ideas. -->
- **The job:** "What were you ultimately trying to get done?"
- **The struggling moment:** "What was the hardest / most frustrating part?"
- **Current workaround:** "How do you handle it today when that happens?"  <!-- the real competitor is the status quo -->
- **Cost of the problem:** "What did that cost you — time, money, stress, a missed outcome?"
- **What 'better' means:** "If that moment had gone perfectly, what would have been different?"
- **Forces (Switch):** "What almost stopped you from changing anything?" <!-- anxiety / habit pulling back -->

## 7. Wrap-up & referral
- "Is there anything I should have asked about that I didn't?"
- "Who else do you know who deals with <this>? Could you introduce me?"  <!-- snowball recruiting -->
- "Can I follow up if I have a question?"

## 8. Do / Don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Ask about the last specific time it happened | Ask "would you use / pay for this?" |
| Let them talk; follow their story | Pitch, demo, or describe your solution |
| Probe past behaviour & workarounds | Ask leading or hypothetical questions |
| Capture exact words / quotes | Accept compliments as evidence |

## 9. Post-interview (within the session, while fresh)
<!-- Synthesise each session fresh — don't batch. Feeds Research_Insights.md. -->
1. Write an **interview snapshot** → Research_Insights.md §5 (who · situation · job · struggle · workaround · quotes).
2. Extract findings as `INS-NN` in the customer's **own words** → Research_Insights.md §3. Never invent a quote.
3. Draft 1-3 job stories ("When… I want… so I can…") → JTBD.md §2; link each to its `INS-*`.
4. Flag any theme resting on <3 sources as **weak**; note disconfirming evidence sought.
